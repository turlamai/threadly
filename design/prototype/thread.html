Performance of for/fold vs named let over large hashes

bbogdan·#1·3d

Nobody's wrong here — the difference only shows once the accumulator is boxed. A named let over hash-ref/hash-set! pays a re-hash on every step, while for/fold keeps the accumulator in a register until the loop body forces it out.

(for/fold ([acc 0])
          ([k (in-hash-keys h)])
  (+ acc (hash-ref h k)))
